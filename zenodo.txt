This paper tests the Temporal Equivalence Principle (TEP) through strong-lensing time-delay residuals, lensing–dynamical mass inference, and action-level photon-path refraction. SN Refsdal offers an unusually high-leverage test case: seven pre-reappearance lens-model variants spanning five modelling families published predictions for the long-baseline SX reappearance delay before the image was observed, and Kelly et al. (2023) later measured that delay independently from SN light-curve fitting. Six of seven strictly blind original predictions and all seven delay-blind revised predictions yield positive residuals (observed delay longer than predicted; correlation-aware family-sign-flip p = 0.031, strictly blind floor p = 0.094). Under a uniform time-delay rescaling, the +30.1 ± 8.9 d residual corresponds to a target scale shift of 8.7%: stellar kinematics break the lens-model mass-sheet degeneracy, making the dynamical–lensing mass difference (phantom mass) a sign-testable consequence of the coupled reconstruction. For the multiply-imaged SLSN-I SN 2025wny (zₛ = 2.015, five images A–E resolved by HST/JWST across a two-galaxy lens), whose delays were measured in August 2026, the predicted residual (+2.3 to +7.1 d on the measured 65.7±3.5 d A–D baseline; Step 36b) is comparable to current lens-model precision (≈ 6.8 d), keeping the out-of-sample proxy test live.

At the action level (Step 64), the optical mechanism is resolved by comparing the two metric realizations on the selected two-branch kinetic sector P_,X=k√ξ+2ξ (k=16.03 fixed by a₀, zero per-system free parameters). Under the canonical unstretched-space temporal metric g̃_μν=-A_loc²(u_grav(x))U_μ U_ν+Aₛₚ²(u_drift(t,x))Π_μν (U_μ≡-∇_μφ̄/√(2X̄)) — where the (3+1) ADM spacetime lapse rescales as N_TEP(x)=A(φ(x))N_GR(x) along the cosmic temporal flow while the spatial 3-metric γ_ij remains unstretched — intermediate clock rates along the photon path do not cancel: every point x contributes its local temporal depth -β_A u_grav(x) directly to the optical refractive index n(x)≃1-2Φ_N(x)/c²-β_A u_grav(x). Sourced by the cosmic baryon fraction alone (f_b=0.16, zero CDM), this unstretched-space temporal channel yields a 2-D sheet Fermat delay Δ t(SX-S1)=+330.9 d and a 3-D deprojected NFW ray delay of +221.6 d (1×) to +443.2 d (2×) in the exact observed arrival order SX>S1>S2>S3>S4, while preserving c_GW=c_γ identically on the shared temporal-lapse cone (Δ t_γ-GW=0) and satisfying Solar-System Shapiro bounds via quadratic-branch screening.

The probative signal is structurally concentrated in the S4–SX contrast: the inner Einstein cross provides negligible probative leverage under the adopted proxy. A transport audit establishes that the archived ψ map measures the ordinary Fermat/Shapiro potential already included in the GR lens predictions; because isotropic g̃_μν=A²g_μν preserves null curves at fixed g_μν, it cannot be counted again as a static conformal residual. A separate hold-out test, calibrated only on SN Encore and the primary SN H0pe contrast with the common observed-delay covariance retained, predicts +116.8±74.5 d for Refsdal, statistically consistent with the covariance-corrected +29.6±10.2 d at 1.16σ. Together with the unstretched-space temporal refraction solve (Step 64), the blind sign result and baryon-sourced delay scale provide a self-consistent optical and timing framework testable on the next long-baseline multiply-imaged supernova.
